How a hash table grows

Keys land in buckets by hash. When the table gets crowded it doubles and re-hashes everything, which is what keeps lookups fast.

Normal run: the table doubles whenever items ÷ buckets passes the threshold.

What to notice

  • Each key goes to bucket hash(key) mod buckets. Two keys in one bucket is a collision, and they form a chain.
  • A resize is expensive (every key moves), but it happens rarely and halves the load factor. Total work stays roughly linear.
  • Notice keys jump to different buckets after a resize, because the modulus changed. Same hash, new home.
  • Turn on Break it: chains grow linearly, so inserts and lookups drift from O(1) toward O(n).
